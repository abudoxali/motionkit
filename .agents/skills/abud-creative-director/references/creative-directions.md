# Creative directions

Directions are systems, not palette swaps. A selected direction must affect copy density, pacing, type behavior, framing, camera, transitions, and audio posture.

| Direction | Typography and composition | Pacing and camera | Transition and color behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Polished | Restrained hierarchy, generous negative space, one claim per shot | Fewer longer shots, controlled push-ins, steady product detail | Soft match cuts or 0.5–0.8s blends; disciplined brand palette |
| Cinematic | Large image-led frames, sparse dramatic lines, deep foreground layers | Wide-to-close escalation, tracking, reveals, motivated parallax | Light/shadow or scale match cuts; contrast arc supports stakes |
| Playful | Friendly type contrast, characterful shapes, visual jokes from product behavior | Bouncy but readable rhythm, reaction shots, elastic follow-through | Shape wipes and object-led handoffs; warm high-clarity color |
| Chaotic | Short emphatic text, deliberate asymmetry, dense staged collisions | Rapid cuts with planned breath points; aggressive but controlled zooms | Hard/flash/zoom cuts used selectively; high-energy accent contrast |
| Deadpan | Sparse copy, quiet frames, precise alignment, intentional emptiness | Long holds and almost-static camera; small motion carries the joke | Minimal cuts or slow dissolves; neutral palette with one dry accent |
| App-store promotional | Product UI is the hero, clean feature framing, concise labels | Smooth vertical travel, phone/device tracking, interaction close-ups | Crisp card or device transitions; accurate product brand colors |
| Minimal editorial | Typographic grid, strong crop, image/type dialogue, no ornamental UI chrome | Measured cuts, subtle rostrum moves, deliberate rhythm changes | Graphic wipes and layout reflow; limited palette with paper/ink logic |

These are starting points, not a menu: mix them, or set them aside when the project needs something else. Custom directions are welcome. Translate them into the same seven decisions: hierarchy, density, pacing, camera, transition, color treatment, and audio posture. Preserve the user's phrase as the creative north star instead of replacing it with a preset label.
