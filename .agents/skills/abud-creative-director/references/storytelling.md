# Hook, story, motion, and preview specification

Use `Hook → Reveal → Demonstration → Payoff → Outro` when it fits. Compress, reorder, or omit beats when the subject needs a different dramatic shape.

## Hook

Plan the first two seconds first. A useful hook is specific to the product and visually legible with sound off. Good sources include:

- A frustrating before-state immediately interrupted by the product.
- A result shown first, followed by how it happened.
- A real interaction performed at an unexpected scale or speed.
- A character consequence that creates a question the next shot answers.

Fast motion does not excuse unreadable copy. Enter quickly, then hold: allow about 0.8 seconds settled for a short label and roughly 0.3 seconds per Arabic word plus 0.6 seconds for a sentence.

## Scene specification

For each scene, record:

- Exact duration and narrative job.
- The visual idea: what the phrase means and how the picture shows it (one sentence).
- Shot size and camera move.
- Character or product blocking, including anticipation, contact, reaction, and follow-through.
- Real product material shown.
- On-screen Arabic/English copy and reading hold.
- Foreground, subject, and background layers.
- Primary visual event and secondary motion.
- Motivated transition into the next scene.
- Optional music energy, SFX cue, or narration timing; write `intentional silence` when appropriate.

Durations must sum to the composition length. Every scene needs a changing relationship, not merely an entering headline. Scenes are review windows, not a required count: one scene may hold several camera moves, and a hard cut can hide inside a continuous move.

## Remotion handoff

- Name the composition, dimensions, FPS, total frames, and review timestamps.
- Identify the engine pieces reused and the product-specific components to create; presets are optional starting points.
- Keep Arabic words whole and RTL layout deliberate.
- Make camera and animation deterministic from the frame clock.
- Review at least one settled and one transitional frame per major beat, then watch the complete Studio playback.
- End by opening Studio and requesting owner notes. A preview is not export approval.
